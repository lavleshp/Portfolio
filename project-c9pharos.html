Overview

C9Pharos is an enterprise cybersecurity platform designed to help organizations monitor website health, SSL certificates, uptime, domain information, vulnerabilities, email breaches, and security intelligence from a single application.

The product evolved from C9Eye to C9Pharos as the platform expanded into a broader cybersecurity ecosystem.

Product Highlights

  • 10,000+ Google Play Downloads
  • 4.5★ Rating (66 Reviews)
  • Redesigned during transition from C9Eye to C9Pharos
